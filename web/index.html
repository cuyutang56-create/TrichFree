Pulling often starts before you notice

Trichotillomania is a body-focused repetitive behaviour: repeatedly pulling out your own hair, even when you want to stop. It is not a lack of willpower, and it is nothing to be ashamed of.

For many people, pulling happens during focused or stressful moments at a screen: studying, working, reading. A hand drifts to the hair, and it can be a while before you realise. TrichFree is built around that moment of noticing, so you can choose what happens next.

TrichFree is a self-help tool, not a medical device or a treatment. It does not diagnose anything. If pulling is affecting your health or wellbeing, please consider speaking with a doctor or a therapist experienced with body-focused repetitive behaviours.

How it works

  1. 1

    Notice

    While you work, TrichFree uses your webcam on your own computer to notice when a hand stays at your hair for a moment. It looks for possible hand-to-hair movements; it cannot tell every pull apart from ordinary touching.

  2. 2

    A gentle reminder

    You choose what happens: a soft chime, a few words in your own voice, or a quiet on-screen note with an optional suggestion like “lower your hand gently”. Never more than two reminders for one movement.

  3. 3

    Make it yours

    Adjust sensitivity, the areas to watch and the quiet time between reminders. Tell it when it got something wrong, and it learns your movements, privately, on your device.

Hear it in your own voice

An alarm can feel like being told off. Your own words land differently. Record a short line, like “Hey, it’s me. Your hand is up there”, or ask someone who cares about you to record one. TrichFree plays it after a soft chime.

Your recordings stay on your device. They are never uploaded.

Private by design

Stays on your device

  • Your camera video. It is analysed frame by frame and never saved or sent.
  • Your voice recordings.
  • Your history of reminders, and what TrichFree learns about your movements (unless you join the research pilot and agree to share pilot data).

Only if you create an account

  • Your email address, to sign in with a one-time code. No password.
  • Your reminder settings, so they follow you to another computer.
  • Reflections, only if you choose to write and save them.

Always in your control

  • Use TrichFree without an account: everything stays on your computer.
  • Download your account data as a file at any time.
  • Delete your account and its data yourself, in one step.

No ads, no trackers, no third-party scripts, and we never sell data. Read the full privacy policy.

Meet Mochi (optional)

Mochi is a small, squishable companion you can switch on once you know your way around. It keeps you company during focus time, celebrates showing up rather than “perfect” days, and is nice to squeeze when your hands want something to do.

About pulling, Mochi is always kind. And if you prefer a quiet tool, simply leave Mochi off.

Our story

TrichFree is a student-led social-impact project. It started from a simple observation: many people with trichotillomania only notice they are pulling after it has happened, and the tools around them, like phone alarms and habit apps, do not see the moment it begins.

We are building something gentle and private: on-device detection, reminders in your own voice, and no judgement. Early testing has already changed the product. A missed pull of a strand held beside the face led us to add hair-aware detection. We are now preparing a small pilot to learn, carefully and honestly, whether TrichFree helps.

TrichFree has no clinical endorsements or partnerships to report. Any results we share will come from our own pilot, clearly labelled.

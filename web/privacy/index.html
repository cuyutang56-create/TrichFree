Privacy policy

Version 2026-10-12. Applies to the TrichFree website, the web account page and the TrichFree browser extension.

TrichFree helps people with trichotillomania notice possible hand-to-hair movements while they work at a computer. Information about hair pulling can reveal a health condition, so we collect as little as possible and keep the most sensitive things on your own device.

The short version

  • Your camera video never leaves your device. It is analysed frame by frame and immediately discarded. No video, image or face data is saved or uploaded, ever.
  • Your voice recordings never leave your device.
  • You can use TrichFree without an account. Then everything stays in your browser.
  • With a free account we store your email address, your reminder settings, and reflections only if you write and save them.
  • You can download or delete your account data yourself, at any time, from the account page or the extension.
  • No ads, no tracking, no third-party scripts, and we never sell or share your data for marketing.

Each account’s data is protected by row-level security in the database: a signed-in user can only ever read or change their own rows.

Pilot interest form

If you register interest in the pilot we store your email address, the role you selected, the optional note you wrote and your confirmations. Only the TrichFree team can read this list. We use it only to contact you about the pilot and delete it within 12 months, or sooner if you ask.

Research pilot (opt-in)

Pilot participants receive an anonymous code from the research team and agree to a separate notice inside the extension. Only then does the extension send: your participant code; the times possible movements were detected; which reminder played; whether your hand came down within 10 seconds; your “Not a pull” and “I just pulled” taps; desk minutes; and calibration quality scores. It never sends video, images or voice. You can stop sending at any time in Settings. The team keeps the link between codes and people offline, separately from this data.

Who processes data for us

  • Supabase hosts the database and handles sign-in. Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS) and at rest by the provider.
  • An email delivery provider sends your one-time sign-in codes.
  • Our website host serves these pages and, like any web server, briefly processes standard request logs such as IP addresses.

These providers keep short-lived security and service logs (for example, sign-in events with an IP address and time).

Your choices and rights

  • Access and portability: download everything your account stores as a JSON file (account page, or Settings in the extension).
  • Correction: change any setting at any time.
  • Deletion: delete your account in one step. This removes your profile, settings and reflections from the database immediately. Copies in the provider’s encrypted backups are removed when those backups expire (typically within days). Data on your computer is removed separately with “Delete all data on this computer”.
  • Withdrawing consent: stop pilot data sharing in Settings, or ask us to remove you from the pilot interest list.

You can also contact your local data protection authority (in Singapore, the Personal Data Protection Commission).

Age

TrichFree is meant for people aged 18 or over, or younger people with a parent or guardian helping them.

Security

We use encrypted connections, row-level security on every account table, least-privilege access, strict content security policies and no third-party scripts. No system is perfectly secure; if we ever learn of a breach affecting your data, we will tell you.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, we will update the version date above and tell account holders before the change applies.

Contact

Questions or requests: email the TrichFree team, or open an issue on our GitHub page (please don’t post personal details there).

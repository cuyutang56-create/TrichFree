## Description
TrichFree Desk is a self-help tool for people with trichotillomania (hair pulling) who spend long hours at a laptop.

- **Your voice, not an alarm.** Record five short lines, like "Hey, it's me. Your hand is up there." When your hand stays at your hair for a moment, you hear a soft chime and one of your own clips.
- **Your zones.** Pick where you usually pull (crown, hairline, sides, brows, lashes) and calibrate in about 5 minutes so everyday moves like drinking or resting your chin don't set it off.
- **Works in the background.** Keep studying or working in any app. Give feedback from a quiet notification or a keyboard shortcut.
- **Private by design.** Detection runs on your computer with Google's MediaPipe. No video or images are saved or sent.
- **Honest progress.** See how often your hand came down within 10 seconds. No streaks, no shame.

TrichFree Desk is not a medical device or treatment. If you swallow hair or feel unwell, please see a doctor.
